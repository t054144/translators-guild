About the data

330 entries compiled by hand: 6 concepts across 55 languages. Speaker counts are first-language estimates rounded to the nearest million, in the range Ethnologue and Wikipedia report.

On romanisation

Transliterations follow the common scholarly scheme for each script (ALA-LC, ISO 15919, Hepburn, Revised Romanisation, Pinyin). They approximate pronunciation — they do not replace it.

Growing it

Every source here is open and bulk-downloadable: Wiktionary extracts, Tatoeba sentence pairs, Wikidata lexemes, and PanLex, which holds over a billion translation pairs.

Reading a card

Coloured dot and label give the language family; the monospace code is its ISO 639-3 identifier. The foot line names the script, writing direction, and native speakers. Click any card to copy the word.
