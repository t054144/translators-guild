What we are designing toward

  • Restricted openings, so the wrong things are harder to post in
  • Separate compartments by device category
  • Heat-resistant construction
  • Temperature or smoke monitoring
  • Automatic alerts when a container needs attention
  • Battery-safety instructions written on the container itself

These are proposed engineering requirements, not confirmed or certified features. No container design or suppression system will be used until it has been reviewed and approved by qualified fire-safety engineers and the appropriate authorities.
