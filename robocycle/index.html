Our idea

It starts with
what we leave behind.

Electronic devices often stay forgotten in our homes or end up as waste. Inside them are batteries, electronic boards, sensors, chips and components that should be handled responsibly.

Instead of letting useful technology become waste, Robocycle gives it another journey.

Old phones Chargers Cables Laptops Electronic boards Small devices Batteries

Batteries need care while they wait

A lithium battery does not become safe by being unused — cells swell and vent as they age. Keep a swollen or hot battery out of the bin and out of the house, keep loose cells apart with their terminals taped, and bring them to us rather than storing them in a drawer.
