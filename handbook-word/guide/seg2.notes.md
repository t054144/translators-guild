# seg2 (Chapters 9–13): issues noticed but not changed

## Arabic
- Ch12, worked example: {{في فندق شيراتون}} adds {{فندق}}, which is not in _at the Sheraton_. The bullet list calls _Sheraton_ → {{شيراتون}} plain borrowing and does not mention the addition (the addition strategy in Ch13 would fit it).
- Ch12, Adaptation example: {{هما كقيسٍ وليلى}} (with tanwīn) vs Ch13 {{هما كقيس وليلى}} (without). Same sentence, inconsistent diacritics.
- Ch9 and Ch12 both use {{التكافؤ}} for two different ideas: equivalence in general (Ch9) and one procedure (Ch12). The term entries tell them apart only by the English "(procedure)" label. Chapter 10's {{التكييف}} (method) and Ch12's {{التكييف}} (procedure) have the same problem.
- Ch12, Calque = {{النسخ}}: accepted, but {{المحاكاة}} / {{الترجمة المستعارة}} are also common. Check this matches the term used in Chapter 16.
- Ch10: {{لقد كان يوماً طويلاً}} is marked ✗ as a literal version. The Arabic is grammatical and understandable, so a beginner may not see why it is wrong. The point (English _long day_ = tiring day) could be stated.

## Factual / consistency
- Ch9 says the last resort is to "give the idea"; Ch13 says "Explanatory translation is the last option". These two "last option" claims may confuse beginners.
- Ch13, cultural substitution: {{الثامنة إلا ثلثاً}} → _7:40_ and {{الثالثة فجراً}} → _3 a.m._ are really changes in format, not cultural substitution in the Romeo and Juliet sense.
- Ch13, "A more general word" (Baker, 2018): the _prawns_/_shrimps_ → {{روبيان}} example is not attributed to Baker in the text. Check whether it comes from the course materials or from Baker.
- Ch11: the four steps are "based on four of the seven standard procedures (Newmark, 1988)". Newmark's list is usually summarised as seven procedures, so this is fine, but the page number is missing.
- Ch10: the Covid-19 and parents examples have no Arabic version, unlike the others. Kept as they were.
