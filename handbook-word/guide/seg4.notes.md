## Arabic
- Ch 24 table: {{علم}} glossed as “flag, knowledge or ‘taught’”. Unvocalised علم reads naturally as عَلَم / عِلْم / عَلِمَ (“knew”); “taught” is عَلَّمَ. Defensible as an unvocalised form, but “knew” may be the clearer third gloss.
- Ch 21: {{أرَجيّ}} for _allergic_ is the Unified Medical Dictionary form; {{تحسّسي}} is far more common in everyday Arabic. Kept as written.
- Ch 22: in the machine-output example, {{لا تفوّت الخروج!}} is presented as an MT error; fine as an illustration, just noting it is invented output.
