# seg3 notes (Chapters 14–20): problems noticed but not changed

## Arabic
- Ch14 try: the question writes {{أثلج صدري}}, but the body example is {{أثلجَ الخبرُ صدري}}. This is fine as a shortened form, but the diacritics are inconsistent.
- Ch14: {{اقلب الجرّة على فمها تطلع البنت لأمّها}} is colloquial (Levantine/Egyptian). The note says so, but a beginner might use it in formal text. You could add a formal option such as {{من شابه أمّه فما ظلم}} (not added, because that would be new content).
- Ch14: {{نام كالقتيل}} is past tense, but the English entry is an infinitive (_to sleep like a log_). The same applies to {{نشيطٌ كالنحلة}} against _as busy as a bee_. This is minor.
- Ch18: {{الوزير يفتتح مستشفى جديداً}}: Arabic news headlines more often use a verbal noun ({{افتتاح مستشفى جديد}}) or a verb-first sentence ({{يفتتح الوزير…}}). The given form is acceptable.
- Ch18: {{السعودية ستمدّد تأشيرات الوافدين}}: the English says "extend visas for expats". The Arabic is fine, but {{تمديد}} as a verbal-noun headline would match the previous bullet's advice.
