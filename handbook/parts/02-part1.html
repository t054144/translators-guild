Translation, communication and the translator

الترجمة والتواصل والمترجم
Key termsمفاهيم أساسية
Translation الترجمة التحريرية
Transferring the meaning of a written text from one language into another.
Interpretation الترجمة الشفهية
Transferring spoken language into another language orally, either consecutively or simultaneously.
Translation Studies علم الترجمة
The academic discipline that studies translation as a process, a product and a social practice.
Communication التواصل
To understand and to be understood: a sender passes a message to a receiver through a channel.

Four ways to define translation

Translation is…What it means for you
Transfer of meaningWe carry the textual meaning across, not the words. Meaning comes before form (Ch. 3).
Interlingual communicationTranslation lets people who share no language communicate. If the reader does not understand, the translation has failed.
A search for equivalenceWe look for equivalence between two languages at several levels: word, phrase, text and function (Ch. 5).
ApproximationNo two languages match exactly, so we bring the SL meaning as close as possible to the TL reader, especially its cultural meaning (Ch. 9).

Three kinds of translation أنواع الترجمة عند ياكوبسون

Jakobson (1959) distinguishes three kinds of translation. This handbook is about the second.

Intralingual
Rewording within one language, as in paraphrasing a legal clause in plain English. ضمن اللغة الواحدة
Interlingual
Translation proper: from one language into another, for example English into Arabic. بين لغتين
Intersemiotic
From words into another sign system, as when a novel becomes a film or a poem becomes a painting. بين نظامين علاميين
01 · Translation, communication and the translatorالترجمة والتواصل والمترجم

The translator inside the communication chain

In ordinary communication, a sender passes a message through a channel (written or oral) to a receiver. Noise is anything that distorts the message. Perceived value is what the receiver actually takes from it. In translation, the chain doubles: the translator is first the receiver of the ST and then the sender of the TT.

Two kinds of translator

Commissioned translator مترجم بتكليفSelf-initiated translator مترجم بمبادرة ذاتية
A client asks you to translate. You work within the client’s policy, house style and ideology.You are your own boss. You choose the text, and you answer for your own choices.

Why the client matters. The same term can be rendered in different ways depending on the editorial policy of the institution that commissions the translation. Examples:

suicide bomber→انتحاري / استشهاديThe choice between these two renderings reflects the commissioning outlet’s editorial stance, not a language rule.
the Persian Gulf→الخليج العربي / الخليج الفارسيPlace names carry political weight. Follow the client’s style guide and flag the issue in your commentary.

The translator’s profile

  • Bilingual: fluent in both the SL and the TL, with a wide linguistic repertoire (الحصيلة اللغوية) and a rich mental lexicon.
  • Bicultural: cultural knowledge that matches linguistic knowledge.
  • Master of idiomaticity and phraseology (Ch. 4).
  • A walking encyclopedia: broad general knowledge, and familiarity with the topic of every text.
  • Experienced: skill that grows only with practice and revision.
  • Skilled with tools: dictionaries, termbases and MT (Part III).

English and Arabic compared

مقارنة بين الإنجليزية والعربية
Key termsمفاهيم أساسية
Derivational language لغة اشتقاقية
A language that builds words from roots and patterns. Arabic is one: from ك-ت-ب come كاتب، مكتوب، مكتبة. English relies more on compounding and affixes (bookshop, writer).
Verbal / nominal sentence جملة فعلية / اسمية
An Arabic sentence that starts with a verb (VSO) or with a noun. English is typically SVO.
Register المستوى اللغوي
The level of formality chosen for a situation, from formal to informal.
Diglossia الازدواجية اللغوية
Two varieties of one language used side by side for different functions, such as MSA and a spoken dialect.

Linguistic and stylistic differences

FeatureEnglishArabic

Sentence lengthShort sentences, separated by full stops.Long sentences linked by و، ف، ثم and commas.
The verb “to be”Always needed: Translation is a field…No verb in present nominal sentences. Use يُعدّ / يُعتبر when a main verb helps: تُعدّ الترجمة مجالاً…
Synonym pairsRare, and usually felt as redundant (outside legal doublets such as null and void).Common for emphasis: شجاعٌ مِغوار, بحرٌ هائجٌ مائج.

Three varieties of Arabic أنواع العربية

Classical

The language of the Qur’an and of pre-Islamic and classical poetry.
Modern Standard

Used in the press, law and education. The default for written translation.
Dialectal

Everyday regional speech (Kuwaiti, Egyptian, Levantine and others). Use it only if the brief allows.

Four areas of difficulty

  1. Grammar (syntax, النحو): word order, tense, agreement, the passive voice.
  2. Semantics (word choice, الدلالة): collocations, connotation, false friends.
  1. Punctuation and style: formal or dialect, and long or short sentences.
  2. General or specialised text: specialised terms need research (Ch. 11–12).

Form, meaning and context

البناء والمعنى والسياق
Key termsمفاهيم أساسية
Form البناء
The structure that carries meaning: a word structure, compound, phrase, clause, sentence, paragraph or text.
Meaning المعنى
The content a form expresses. It is cognitive (mental and abstract), and that is why it is complex.
Denotation المعنى الإشاري / المعجمي
The core, conceptual dictionary meaning of a word.
Connotation المعنى الإيحائي / الضمني
The associations and shades of meaning added to the denotation: positive, negative, formal, religious, political.

The golden rule: meaning over form

Behind every form there is a meaning. You may change the form. You may not change the meaning.

Because English and Arabic build meaning with different structures, changing the form is normal. An adjective can become a verb, or a verb a noun, and so on (see transposition, Ch. 7).

Blair, Bush’s poodle→بلير، ذيلُ بوش / تابعُ بوشA poodle has no political sense in Arabic كلب بوش المدلّل. The meaning, “an obedient follower”, is kept and the image changes.
He is a heavy smoker.→يدخّن بشراهةAdjective + noun becomes verb + adverbial.

More key termsمفاهيم أساسية
Sentence meaning
What the words literally say.
Speaker meaning
What the speaker intends by saying them.
Context السياق
Any background information that helps us understand a text.
Homonymy المشترك اللفظي
One form with several unrelated meanings.
Polysemy تعدّد المعنى
One form with several related meanings.

Sentence meaning and speaker meaning

أشعرُ بالحرّ.→Speaker meaning: Please turn on the air conditioning.
It’s freezing in here.→Speaker meaning: أغلق النافذة / أطفئ المكيّف

Keep the form wherever the TL reader can infer the same intention. Make the intention explicit only when they cannot.

Context: micro and macro

Micro context (co-text, the immediate context) is the text itself: the words and sentences around the problem.

Macro context is everything outside the text: the author (Shawqi, Qabbani and Darwish do not write alike), the audience, the translator, the time and place of writing, and the economic, social and historical circumstances.

لكلّ مقامٍ مقال

Every communicative situation has its own context.

The translation triangle: text, author and audience, with the translator ● at the centre.

A fixed expression of more than one word whose total meaning is not the sum of its words: It’s a piece of cake means “it is easy”.

Equivalence

التكافؤ
Key termsمفاهيم أساسية
Equivalence التكافؤ
A central notion in Translation Studies: the relationship between an ST item and the TT item that stands for it. Translation seeks equivalence at several levels (Baker 2018): word, phrase, grammar, text and pragmatics.
Formal التكافؤ الشكلي
Keeps the SL structure: passive for passive, verb for verb, image for image.
Functional التكافؤ الوظيفي
Captures the function of the SL expression without its form, using a TL expression that does the same job.
Ideational التكافؤ الفكري
Conveys only the basic idea, plainly, with neither the SL form nor a TL image.
Dynamic التكافؤ الدينامي
Nida’s (1964) term for a TT that produces the same effect on its readers as the ST had on its own readers.

One sentence, three equivalents EN → AR

The agreement has remained a dead letter since then. (Farghal & Shunnaq 1999; Farghal 2012)

SourceFormalFunctionalIdeational
Think outside the box.فكّر خارج الصندوقفكّر بطريقة إبداعية / غير تقليدية
Ali is second to none in poetry.عليٌّ ثانٍ لا أحدعليٌّ لا يُشقّ له غبار في الشعرعليٌّ شاعرٌ متميّز
Which one? Formal equivalence is not always wrong. فكّر خارج الصندوق has now entered Arabic, and binding legal texts stay close to the form. Informative and persuasive texts usually need a functional equivalent. Use an ideational one when no TL image fits or the reader needs plain language. Also mind your terms: agreement اتفاقية is not the same as treaty معاهدة.
