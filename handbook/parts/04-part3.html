Terminology management

إدارة المصطلحات وأدلّة المصطلحات
Key termsمفاهيم أساسية
Term مصطلح
A word or expression that names a concept within a specialised field. Examples are provided that in law and greenhouse effect in science.
Terminology علم المصطلح
The set of terms in a field, and the discipline that studies them. Every field, and every branch of linguistics, has its own terms.
Termbase قاعدة المصطلحات
A structured database of term records, usually one record per concept.
Terminology guide دليل المصطلحات
A project or client document that lists approved, admitted and forbidden terms, together with style rules.

Five principles

  1. One concept, one entry. Organise by concept, not by word.
  2. One term per concept throughout a text. Never vary a term for style.
  3. Cite a source for every term, and record its context.
  4. Mark the status of each term: preferred, admitted or deprecated.
  5. Share and update the list, so the whole team uses the same terms.

The terminology workflow

Where to find approved Arabic terms

UNTERM (United Nations) · WIPO Pearl (science and technology) · Arabterm (ALECSO and GIZ, technical fields) · the lexicons of the Arabic language academies in Cairo, Damascus and Amman · your client’s own glossaries and style guide.

Tools: a shared spreadsheet is enough to begin. Termbases in CAT tools such as Trados MultiTerm or memoQ flag terms as you type, and the TBX format lets termbases move between tools.

The triangulation method

  1. Find candidate equivalents in a bilingual dictionary.
  2. Confirm each one in a TL monolingual dictionary.
  3. Check real usage in a corpus or parallel text.
  4. Check a specialised source, then record the term.
Example · fast colour
Bilingual: سريع، صائم، ثابت
Monolingual: ثابت = does not fade
Corpus: ألوان ثابتة in textile texts
Decision: لون ثابت

Machine translation and AI

الترجمة الآلية والذكاء الاصطناعي
Key termsمفاهيم أساسية
Machine translation الترجمة الآلية
Translation produced automatically by software.
NMT الترجمة الآلية العصبية
Neural MT: a neural network trained on millions of human translations predicts the TT. It is the basis of most modern MT engines.
LLM النماذج اللغوية الكبيرة
A large language model: general AI systems (chat assistants) that can translate, rewrite and explain on instruction.
CAT tool · TM أدوات الترجمة بمساعدة الحاسوب · ذاكرة الترجمة
Software that stores your past translations (TM) and terms (TB) so you can reuse them. It supports the translator rather than replacing them.
Post-editing التحرير اللاحق
A human editing MT output to reach an agreed level of quality (ISO 18587).
Hallucination الهلوسة
AI output that is fluent but not supported by the ST: added facts, invented names, missing negations.

From rules to neural networks

Where MT fits in a professional workflow

MT is useful for…
  • getting the gist and first drafts of repetitive, informative texts
  • large volumes on a tight deadline
  • brainstorming alternative phrasings
MT is risky for…
  • legal, medical and religious texts
  • poetry, humour, advertising and idioms
  • confidential or personal material

Post-editing: two levels (ISO 18587)

Light post-editing
The TT must be accurate and understandable, but style can be imperfect. It suits internal use or a quick gist.
Full post-editing
The TT must reach publishable quality, comparable to human translation. It suits anything public, such as the press, clients and print.

Using AI responsibly

  • Confidentiality first. Never paste a client’s confidential text into a public MT or AI tool without permission.
  • Disclose the use of MT or AI to your client, and follow any rules they set.
  • You are responsible for every word you deliver, whatever tool produced the draft.
  • Instruct precisely: give the audience, the register, the variety of Arabic and your glossary.
  • Verify everything, especially terms, names, numbers and negations.
  • Keep your skills sharp. Post-editing needs a translator’s judgement, and assessing translation quality is now a core professional skill.

A well-written AI instruction

Translate the press release below from English into Modern Standard Arabic for readers in the Gulf. Use a formal news register and verb-first sentences. Use these terms: greenwashing = التضليل البيئي; Home Office = وزارة الداخلية البريطانية. Do not add, omit or summarise anything. List any ambiguous phrases separately.

Guild tip. Treat MT as a very fast, very confident intern. It is often useful, sometimes brilliant, and never to be trusted without checking.
