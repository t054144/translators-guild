“Behind every form there is a meaning. Change the form if you must, but never the meaning.”

The Guild Translation Handbook is a compact, practical companion for translating between English and Arabic, in both directions. It takes students from the foundations of meaning, context and equivalence through the translator’s methods, procedures and strategies, and on to the tools and practice of the profession: terminology, dictionaries, machine translation, text analysis, critical discourse analysis, commentary and risk assessment.
