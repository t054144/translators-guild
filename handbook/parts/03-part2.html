Types of translation

أنواع الترجمة
Key termsمفاهيم أساسية
Translation method منهج الترجمة
An approach applied to a whole text. It decides how close the TT stays to the SL or to the TL.
Semantic translation الترجمة الدلالية
Stays close to the author’s meaning and style, within what the TL allows (Newmark 1988).
Communicative translation الترجمة التواصلية
Aims for the effect on the reader. The TT reads naturally and is easy to understand.
Adaptation الترجمة التكييفية / الاقتباس
The freest method: themes and plot are kept, and the culture is converted. It is common for plays, songs and children’s books.

Adapted from Newmark (1988: 45–47).

Types by medium and purpose

TypeWhat it is
Written translation تحريريةFrom one written text into another, e.g. legal, literary, technical, medical, media and religious texts.
Sight translation منظورةReading a written text aloud directly in another language, as in court or hospital settings.
Consecutive interpreting تتابعيةThe speaker pauses and the interpreter renders each segment, often with notes.
Simultaneous interpreting فوريةInterpreting while the speaker talks, usually from a booth.
Audiovisual translation سمعية بصريةSubtitling, dubbing and voice-over, with limits of time and space.
Localisation التوطينAdapting software, websites or games to a locale: language, dates, currency, layout direction.
Transcreation الترجمة الإبداعيةRecreating advertising or branding so it has the same emotional effect in the TL.
Back-translation الترجمة العكسيةTranslating a TT back into the SL to check its accuracy, as with medical questionnaires.
MT + post-editingA human corrects machine output (Ch. 13).

Literal translation الترجمة الحرفية المعدّلة

Farghal calls it modified literal translation: the SL words are kept, with only the grammatical adjustments the TL needs. It can be successful or destructive.

Successful: a TL equivalent exists
play a role→يلعب دوراً
The book is on the table.→الكتاب على الطاولة
Destructive: no TL equivalent
pay a visit→يدفع زيارة يقوم بزيارة
a long day→يوم طويل يوم شاق

Context: in a bridal advertisement, What are you wearing on your biggest day? means ماذا سترتدين في يوم زفافك؟ Note the feminine verb, and أهمّ يوم لديك is wrong here.

Free translation الترجمة الحرّة

Free translation (also called loose translation) keeps the message but not the manner, and it is used for all kinds of text, not only literary ones. Some scholars do not count heavily adapted texts as translations at all, only as new texts.

SourceFree renderingVerdict
Appointing new employees should be based on equal opportunity.يجب أن يتمّ تعيين الموظفين الجدد بعيداً عن المحسوبية والواسطةWorks The same message, made local.
We have had heavy rainfall this winter.سيكون لدينا موسم زراعي مبشّر هذا العامToo free Adds a conclusion the ST never states.

Seven procedures and beyond

إجراءات الترجمة السبعة (فيني وداربلنيه)
Key termsمفاهيم أساسية
Direct translation الترجمة المباشرة
Used when SL elements can be carried over closely: borrowing, calque and literal translation.
Oblique translation الترجمة غير المباشرة
Used when the structures or concepts differ: transposition, modulation, equivalence and adaptation.

Vinay and Darbelnet (1958/1995) set out seven procedures, from the closest to the SL to the freest. Naming them lets you justify each decision in your commentary.

Method, strategy or procedure?

TermScopeExample
Method منهجThe whole text (Ch. 6)Translating a news report communicatively
Strategy استراتيجيةA plan for a translation problem (Ch. 8)Cultural substitution for an idiom
Procedure إجراءA local technique for a word or phrase (Ch. 7)Transposing a noun into a verb

Direct procedures

ProcedureDefinitionExamples
1 · BorrowingThe SL word is taken over as it is, transliterated.computer → كمبيوتر · radio → راديو
ديوانية → diwaniya · صكوك → sukuk
2 · CalqueA loan translation: the parts of an SL expression are translated one by one.skyscraper → ناطحة سحاب · shadow government → حكومة الظل · brainwashing → غسيل الدماغ
3 · LiteralWord-for-word translation that is still grammatical and natural in the TL.The book is on the table. → الكتاب على الطاولة
07 · Seven procedures and beyondإجراءات الترجمة السبعة

Oblique procedures

ProcedureDefinitionExamples
4 · TranspositionThe word class changes but the meaning does not, e.g. noun to verb, or adjective to adverb.after his arrival → بعد أن وصل
a heavy smoker → يدخّن بشراهة
لدى وصوله → when he arrived
5 · ModulationThe point of view changes, e.g. negative to positive, passive to active, or part to whole.It is not difficult to… → من السهل أن…
I don’t blame you. → معك حق
The window was broken by Ali. → كسر عليٌّ النافذة
6 · EquivalenceThe same situation is described with a completely different expression. It is typical of idioms, proverbs and interjections.Like father, like son. → هذا الشبل من ذاك الأسد
Ouch! → آخ!
7 · AdaptationAn SL situation unknown in the TL culture is replaced by one that is familiar there.like Romeo and Juliet → كقيسٍ وليلى
a summer’s day (Shakespeare) → يوم ربيعي (Ch. 9)

Four procedures in one sentence EN → AR

After his arrival in Kuwait, the minister held a press conference at the Sheraton and said the reforms were not impossible.

بعد أن وصل الوزير إلى الكويت، عقد مؤتمراً صحفياً في فندق شيراتون، وقال إنّ الإصلاحات ممكنة.

■ after his arrival → بعد أن وصلTransposition: noun to verb
■ press conference → مؤتمر صحفيCalque, now fully established
■ Sheraton → شيراتونBorrowing (a proper name)
■ not impossible → ممكنةModulation: double negative to positive

Translation strategies

استراتيجيات الترجمة
Key termsمفاهيم أساسية
Strategy استراتيجية
A planned solution to a translation problem, especially when there is no direct equivalent (non-equivalence).
Domestication التوطين
Bringing the text closer to TL culture so it reads as if originally written in the TL (Venuti 1995).
Foreignisation التغريب
Keeping the foreign flavour of the ST so that the reader notices its origin.
Compensation التعويض
Making up for a loss at one point, such as a lost pun or rhyme, with a similar effect elsewhere in the TT.

The strategy toolbox

StrategyWhen to use itExample
Functional translationA TL expression with the same function exists.spill the beans → أفشى السرّ
Cultural substitution الإبدال الثقافيReplace a culture-specific item with a TL item that has a similar effect.like Romeo and Juliet → كقيسٍ وليلى
Addition / explicitationMostly EN → AR. Arabic favours elaboration (لغة إسهاب), and readers may need background.the Fed → مجلس الاحتياطي الفيدرالي (البنك المركزي الأمريكي)
Omission الحذفMostly AR → EN: cut synonym pairs, repetition and words that add nothing. Also used, if the client agrees, to avoid offending TL readers.شجاعٌ مِغوار → very brave
brave and daring
More general word (superordinate)The TL lacks the specific word.prawns / shrimps → روبيان
Loan word + explanationA culture-bound term that needs to be kept.الوقف → waqf (an Islamic charitable endowment)
Explanatory / descriptive الشرحيةThe last resort, for cultural and technical words (Ch. 10).الزكاة، العِدّة، الصدقة
Footnote / glossExplanation is needed but would break the flow of the text.Common in literary and religious texts
Foreignised تغريب
“They spent the evening at the diwaniya.” The Kuwaiti flavour is kept, and a gloss is added the first time the word appears.
Domesticated توطين
“They spent the evening at a men’s gathering.” The text flows smoothly, but the local colour is lost.

Baker (2018) sets out the classic strategies for non-equivalence at word level. Farghal (2012) discusses the strategies most often used between English and Arabic.

08 · Translation strategiesاستراتيجيات الترجمة

Translating metaphor ترجمة الاستعارة

Key termsمفاهيم أساسية
Figurative language اللغة المجازية
Using words in a non-literal sense. It is a core concern of rhetoric (البلاغة).
Metaphor الاستعارة
A likeness stated without “as” or “like”: She is a moon. In Arabic terms: استخدام اللفظ في غير ما وُضع له.
Simile التشبيه
A likeness stated with “as” or “like”: She is as beautiful as the moon.

Work down this ladder and stop at the first step that works.

1
Shared image → reproduce it. The first choice.
She is a snake.→إنها أفعى
My thoughts evaporated.→تبخّرت أفكاري
My paper will see the light next December.→سترى ورقتي البحثية النور في ديسمبر المقبل
2
Different image → replace it with a TL image.
the tip of the iceberg→غيضٌ من فيض / وما خفي كان أعظمNegative contexts. For a hopeful beginning: أوّل الغيث قطرة
the apple of my eye→قُرّة عيني
3
Convert to a simile, keeping the image and making it explicit.
He is a rock.→إنه كالصخرة في ثباته
4
Reduce to the sense. The last resort, because the image is lost.
He is a chicken.→إنه جبان
The decision raised eyebrows.→أثار القرار الاستغراب

The same ladder, AR → EN

أعطاه الضوء الأخضرgave him the green light1 · shared
هو بحرٌ في العلمhe is a walking encyclopedia2 · TL image
رفع رؤوسناhe made us proud4 · sense
Poetry and prose. Prose leaves little room for change. Poetry allows more creative freedom, since the translator must recreate its expressive and aesthetic effect, not just its content. See the poetry exercises on p. 36.

Approximation التقريب
Bringing the SL meaning as close as possible to the TL, since a perfect match is rare.
Cultural approximation التقريب الثقافي
Approximating cultural meaning. It needs cultural knowledge of both the SLC and the TLC.
Culture-specific item عنصر ثقافي خاص
A word, custom, institution or expression tied to one culture, with no ready counterpart in the other.

09 · Cultural approximationالتقريب الثقافي

When the climate changes the meaning

In English, warmth is comforting. In the Arab world, where the heat is harsh, relief is cold.

It warmed my heart.→أثلجَ صدري

A literal أدفأ قلبي misses the effect. The TL image is reversed so that the feeling stays the same.

Case study · Shakespeare, Sonnet 18

Shall I compare thee to a summer’s day?
Thou art more lovely and more temperate…

Literalأأشبّهكِ بيومٍ من أيام الصيف؟
Approximatedأأشبّهكِ بيومٍ ربيعيّ؟

An English summer is mild and lovely, but a Gulf summer is scorching. Many translators move the image to spring.

Descriptive translation and lexical creation

الترجمة الوصفية والنحت المعجمي
Key termsمفاهيم أساسية
Descriptive translation الترجمة الوصفية / الشرحية
An explanatory strategy: the SL item is paraphrased in the TL by describing its concept. It is a last resort.
Concept مفهوم
A complex meaning, often cultural, religious or technical, that a single word stands for.
Semantic density الكثافة الدلالية
A lot of meaning packed into one word. The denser the word, the more description it needs.

Two steps

  1. Transliterate the SL word: زكاة → zakat.
  2. Describe its meaning in the TL, either in brackets after the word or in a footnote.

Culture-bound Arabic terms AR → EN

الزكاةzakat: obligatory almsgiving in Islam, paid yearly on wealth above a set threshold
التيمّمtayammum: dry ablution with clean earth when water is unavailable
البسملةthe basmala: saying “In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful”
الحوقلةthe hawqala: saying “There is no power nor strength except through God”
العِدّةʿidda: the waiting period a woman observes after divorce or widowhood before remarrying
الاستتابةistitaba: a formal call to repent, issued before a legal penalty is applied
العطوةʿatwa: a temporary truce under tribal customary law (Jordan and the Levant)
الديوانيةdiwaniya: a regular Kuwaiti social gathering held in a home’s reception hall

English culture-bound terms EN → AR

Lexical creation النحت المعجمي / التوليد

Key termsمفاهيم أساسية
Lexical creation (coinage)
The deliberate coining of new words or expressions in the TL for culture-specific, technical or conceptual SL items that have no direct equivalent, because of a lexical gap in the TL.
Lexicalisation التعجيم
The point at which a coinage is accepted and enters the dictionary.
Arabicisation التعريب
Adapting a borrowed word to Arabic sounds and morphology, as with رقمنة for digitisation.

Concepts rooted in religion, politics, technology or culture often have no ready TL word. In those cases, the translator becomes a coiner of new lexical items.

TypeHow it worksExamples
NeologismA new word, often built from an Arabic root.blog → مدوّنة · digitisation → رقمنة
Calque (loan translation)Morpheme-by-morpheme translation: a modified literal translation.smartphone → الهاتف الذكي · global warming → الاحترار العالمي · shadow government → حكومة الظل
TransliterationA phonetic borrowing, with a short definition the first time it appears.radio → راديو · internet → إنترنت
HybridTranslation mixed with transliteration.cybersecurity → الأمن السيبراني
ExplanationA short descriptive phrase that settles into use.jet lag → إرهاق السفر الجوي · co-wife ← الضرّة
