When climate changes meaning

SourceThe news warmed my heart.
Targetأثلجَ الخبرُ صدري
Why: in a hot climate, relief feels cold. The image is reversed so the feeling stays the same.
Example · poetry
Shall I compare thee to a summer’s day? (Shakespeare, Sonnet 18)
In England, summer is mild and lovely. In the Gulf, summer is harsh. Many translators move the image to spring: أأشبّهكِ بيومٍ ربيعيّ؟

Key takeaway
Translate greetings and condolences with the phrase a TL speaker would actually use in the same situation, never word by word.

Descriptive translation

الترجمة الوصفية / الشرحية
Team notes
Concept
Descriptive translation الترجمة الوصفية
Explaining an SL word by describing its meaning in the TL. It is also called explanatory translation.
Concept المفهوم
A complex meaning that a single word stands for.

Use it for cultural, religious and technical words that have no equivalent. It is the last option, because it makes the text longer.

Two steps

1 · Transliterate the word
زكاة → zakat
2 · Describe it briefly
in brackets, or in a footnote

Arabic culture-bound words AR → EN

الزكاةzakat: obligatory almsgiving in Islam, paid yearly when certain conditions are met
التيمّمtayammum: ritual washing with clean earth when water is unavailable
البسملةthe basmala: saying “In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful”
العِدّةʿidda: the waiting period after divorce or the husband’s death before a woman may remarry
الصكوكsukuk: Islamic financial certificates, often called “Islamic bonds”
العطوةʿatwa: a temporary truce under tribal customary law

English culture-bound words EN → AR

Ali has been sectioned. (UK)أُودِع عليٌّ مستشفى للأمراض النفسية بموجب القانون
yin and yangالين واليانغ (قوّتان متضادّتان متكاملتان في الفكر الصيني)
Common error
Mixing up psychology (علم النفس) and psychiatry (الطبّ النفسي). Check what the term means before you explain it.
Key takeaway
Transliterate + explain once. After the first mention, the transliterated word alone is enough.

Lexical creation

النحت المعجمي / توليد الألفاظ
Team notes
Concept
Lexical creation النحت المعجمي
Creating a new word or expression in the TL for something that has no name there yet (a “lexical gap”).
Lexicalisation التعجيم
The moment a new word is accepted and enters the dictionary.
Arabicisation التعريب
Adapting a borrowed word to Arabic sounds and word patterns.

Sometimes the translator becomes a coiner: someone who makes new words.

Five ways to create a word

TypeHow it worksExample

shadow government → حكومة الظل

New words in the news

greenwashingالتضليل البيئيCompanies pretending their products are eco-friendly
cancel cultureثقافة الإلغاءPublicly dropping support for someone as a punishment
global warmingالاحترار العالميAn established calque

Sourceproduct cannibalism (marketing)
Targetمنتجٌ يزاحم منتجاً آخر للشركة نفسها
Why: the literal meaning (eating human flesh) is absurd here. The field of marketing tells you the real meaning.
Translator’s note
Search before you coin. Check the Arabic language academies, UNTERM and Arabterm (Ch. 13). An accepted term is almost always better than a new invention.

Terminology management

Concept
Term المصطلح
A word that names a concept in a specialised field. Every field has its own terms.
Terminology guide دليل المصطلحات
A list of the approved terms for a project or client.

Four rules

  • Be consistent: one term for one concept, throughout the text.
  • Record the context, not just the word.
  • Cite a source for every term.
  • Mark the status: preferred, allowed or avoid.

Where to check Arabic terms

UNTERM (United Nations)
Arabterm (technical fields)
The Arabic language academies (Cairo, Damascus, Amman)
The client’s own glossary and style guide
Common error
Changing a term halfway through “for variety”. In specialised texts, a new word suggests a new concept.
