Equivalence

التكافؤ
Team notesVerify: source of the three-type model (likely Farghal & Shunnaq 1999)
Concept
Equivalence التكافؤ
A central idea in Translation Studies: finding the TL expression that matches the SL one.
Formal الشكلي
Keeps the form of the SL: the same structure, word class and image.
Functional الوظيفي
Keeps the function by using a TL expression that does the same job.
Ideational الفكري
Keeps only the basic idea, in plain words.

One sentence, three equivalents

The agreement has remained a dead letter since then.

More examples

SourceFormalFunctionalIdeational
Ali is second to none in poetry.علي ثانٍ لا أحدعليٌّ لا يُشقّ له غبار في الشعرعليٌّ شاعرٌ متميّز
Think outside the box.فكّر خارج الصندوقفكّر بطريقة إبداعية
Translator’s note
Formal equivalence is not always wrong. فكّر خارج الصندوق is now common in Arabic, and legal texts often stay close to the form. Choose the option your reader will understand.
Common error
Mixing up terms: agreement is اتفاقية, but treaty is معاهدة.
Key takeaway
When the form does not work, keep the function. If no idiom fits, keep the idea.

Collocation, idiomaticity and phraseology

التلازم اللفظي والتعابير الاصطلاحية
Team notesAdded: Firth (1957)
Concept
Collocation التلازم اللفظي
Words that naturally go together: “the company a word keeps” (Firth 1957).
Idiom التعبير الاصطلاحي
A fixed expression whose meaning is not the sum of its words: a piece of cake = “very easy”.
Phraseology التعابير المسكوكة
The fixed and semi-fixed word groups of a language, including collocations, idioms and proverbs.

Types of translation

أنواع الترجمة
Team notes: literal, freeAdded: further types (Newmark 1988)

Some types stay close to the source text. Others move closer to the reader.

Literal translation الترجمة الحرفية (المعدّلة)

Literal translation keeps the words and adjusts only the grammar. It can be successful or destructive.

Works: a TL equivalent exists
play a role → يلعب دوراً
The book is on the table. → الكتاب على الطاولة
Fails: no TL equivalent
pay a visit → يدفع زيارة (use يقوم بزيارة)
a long day → يوم طويل (use يوم شاقّ)

Free translation الترجمة الحرّة

Free translation keeps the message but not the form. Some specialists say a very free version is a new text, not a translation.

SourceAppointing new employees should be based on equal opportunity.
Targetيجب أن يتمّ تعيين الموظفين الجدد بعيداً عن المحسوبية والواسطة.
Works: the same message, in words local readers know.
SourceWe have had heavy rainfall this winter.
Targetسيكون لدينا موسم زراعي مبشّر.
Too free: it adds a conclusion that the source never states.

By medium

Sight translationReading a written text aloud in another language
Consecutive / simultaneousInterpreting after the speaker pauses, or while the speaker talks
AudiovisualSubtitling and dubbing, with limits on time and space
Localisation · TranscreationAdapting websites and apps · recreating adverts for a new market

Translation strategies

استراتيجيات الترجمة
Team notesAdded: superordinate, loan word, compensation, domestication (Baker 2018; Venuti 1995)
Concept
Strategy استراتيجية
A planned solution to a translation problem, especially when there is no direct equivalent.
Functional translationالترجمة الوظيفية

Use whena TL expression does the same job.

spill the beans → أفشى السرّ

Riskthe tone may shift slightly.

Cultural substitutionالإبدال الثقافي

Use whena cultural reference would mean nothing to TL readers.

like Romeo and Juliet → كقيسٍ وليلى

Riskyou lose the original culture. Alternativeadd a short explanation.

Additionالإضافة

Use whenreaders need background. It is common from English into Arabic, a language of elaboration (لغة إسهاب).

the Fed → مجلس الاحتياطي الفيدرالي الأمريكي

Risknever add to titles or headlines.

Omissionالحذف

Use whenwords repeat meaning. It is common from Arabic into English.

شجاعٌ مِغوار → very brave

Risknever omit real information.

More general wordاللفظ الأعمّ

Use whenthe TL has no specific word.

prawns / shrimps → روبيان

Risksome detail is lost.

Loan word + explanationالاقتراض مع الشرح

Use whena culture-bound term must stay.

الوقف → waqf (an Islamic charitable endowment)

Risktoo many explanations slow the reader.

Translator’s note
Explanatory (descriptive) translation is the last option. Use it for cultural and technical words such as الزكاة، العِدّة. See Ch. 11.

Compensationالتعويض

Use whenan effect (a pun, a rhyme, a tone) is lost at one point.

Recreate a similar effect elsewhere in the text.

Riskadding effects the author never used.

Domestication · Foreignisationالتوطين · التغريب

Domesticate: make the text feel local. Foreignise: keep its foreign flavour (Venuti 1995).

“the diwaniya” (foreignised) · “a men’s evening gathering” (domesticated)

Translating metaphor الترجمة المجازية

Concept
Metaphor الاستعارة
A comparison without “as” or “like”: She is a moon.
Simile التشبيه
A comparison with “as” or “like”: She is as beautiful as the moon.

Go down the ladder and stop at the first step that works.

1
Is the image shared by both languages? Keep it.
She is a snake.→إنها أفعى
My paper will see the light next December.→سترى ورقتي البحثية النور في ديسمبر المقبل
2
Is it not shared? Use an Arabic image instead.
the tip of the iceberg→غيضٌ من فيضFor a positive beginning, use أوّل الغيث قطرة
3
No image fits? Turn it into a simile.
He is a rock.→إنه كالصخرة في ثباته
4
Last resort: give the plain meaning. The image is lost.
He is a chicken.→إنه جبان
The decision raised eyebrows.→أثار القرار الاستغراب
Key takeaway
Keep the image if you can, replace it if you must, and explain it only as a last resort. Poetry allows more creative freedom than prose.

The seven translation procedures

إجراءات الترجمة السبعة
Requested in the team notesAdded framework: Vinay & Darbelnet (1958/1995)

A procedure (إجراء) is a technique for translating a word, a phrase or a sentence. The seven procedures run from the one closest to the source (1) to the freest (7).

DIRECT
OBLIQUE
1Borrowingالاقتراض
What it doesTakes the SL word as it is.WhenNew objects and names

What it doesWord for word, and still correct in the TL.WhenThe two structures match

Key takeaway
Start with the direct procedures (1–3). Move to the oblique ones (4–7) only when the direct result sounds unnatural or unclear.
