How to use this handbook

كيف تستخدم هذا الدليل

This is a field handbook: keep it next to your laptop while you translate. It follows the order in which a translator actually works. Part I covers what to do before translating. Parts II and III cover the decisions you make while translating. Parts IV and V cover how to analyse, justify and check your work. Part VI is for practice.

1 · AnalyseحلّلClient, purpose, audience, genre, terms, problems. Ch. 2, 14, 17
2 · TranslateترجمChoose an equivalent, a strategy or a procedure for each problem. Ch. 5–13
3 · CommentعلّقJustify, revise, and assess the risks. Ch. 15, 16, 20
