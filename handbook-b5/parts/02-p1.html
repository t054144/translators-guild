The translator’s foundation

أسس الترجمة والمترجم
Team notes
Concept
Translation الترجمة التحريرية
Transferring the meaning of a written text from one language into another.
Interpretation الترجمة الشفهية
Transferring spoken language into another language orally.
Communication التواصل
To understand and to be understood. Translation is communication between two languages.

Four ways to think about translation

1 · Transfer of meaning
We carry the meaning across, not the words.
2 · Communication
If the reader does not understand, the translation has failed.
3 · A search for equivalence
We look for the TL expression that matches the SL one (Ch. 5).
4 · Approximation
No two languages match exactly, so we get as close as possible (Ch. 10).

Where the translator stands

Two kinds of translator

Commissioned (بتكليف): a client asks you to translate, so you follow the client’s policy and style.

Self-initiated (بمبادرة ذاتية): no client. You choose the text yourself, such as a poem you love, and answer for your own choices.

Note: a freelancer is self-employed but still works for clients, so a freelancer is a commissioned translator.

What a good translator needs

  • Two languages: bilingual
  • Two cultures: bicultural
  • Wide general knowledge, like a “walking encyclopedia”
  • A rich vocabulary (الحصيلة اللغوية)
  • Practice, and the habit of revising
Key takeaway
A translator translates meaning for a reader, never just words.

Translation preparation

نصائح وتقنيات وإعداد للترجمة
Team notesAdded: the translation brief (Nord 1997)

Before you translate a single word, answer three questions. Together they make up your translation brief (موجز الترجمة).

Who is the client?من هو العميل؟Who asked for the translation? What style and policy do they follow?
What is the purpose?ما الغرض التواصلي؟Is the text meant to inform, persuade, instruct or entertain?
Who is the audience?من هو الجمهور المستهدف؟Experts or the general public? Adults or children?

Six preparation steps

  1. Read the whole text twice.
  2. Break it down into paragraphs, then sentences, then phrases.
  3. Highlight difficult words, idioms and names.
  4. Research the topic.
  5. Make a word list of key terms (Ch. 13).
  6. Picture a first draft in your mind.

Five golden habits

  • Be faithful (الأمانة) to the meaning.
  • Aim for a translation that is accurate, faithful and acceptable.
  • Translation is rewriting. It must read naturally.
  • Always revise, even your best work.
  • Translate everything, including titles and captions.
Translator’s note
Translation is like a minefield (الترجمة حقل ألغام). One wrong word can cause real harm. If you are not sure, check. Never guess.

A brief in action Example

TextA short news report about Kuwait and Australia
ClientKUNA (Kuwait News Agency). Follow its house style and approved names.
PurposeTo inform. Keep the language clear, formal and neutral.
AudienceEnglish readers interested in Kuwait and the Middle East
So…Use formal register, no short forms, and keep the headline short with nothing added.
Common error
Starting to translate before reading the whole text. You then miss the main idea and the words that keep coming back.

Arabic vs. English

مقارنة بين العربية والإنجليزية
Team notes
Concept
Verbal sentence الجملة الفعلية
A sentence that starts with a verb. Formal Arabic prefers it: verb – subject – object (VSO).
Register المستوى اللغوي
How formal or informal the language is.

The main differences

EnglishArabic
Word orderSubject first: Sam ate the cake.Verb first: أكلَ سامٌ الكعكةَ
SentencesShort, with full stopsLong, joined with و، ف، ثمّ
The verb “is”Always writtenOften dropped, or rendered يُعدّ
SynonymsOne word is enoughPairs add emphasis: شجاعٌ مِغوار

Small words, big traps EN → AR

EnglishArabicWhy
He is in bed.إنه نائمIt means “asleep”, not في السرير
As an officer, I…بصفتي ضابطاً…Here as means a role, not كـ
Translation is a fascinating field.تُعدّ الترجمة مجالاً شائقاًStart with a verb
…to see the Roman ruinsلزيارة الآثار الرومانيةArabic says “visit”

The other direction AR → EN

Sourceهو شجاعٌ مِغوار.
TargetHe is very brave.
Why: Arabic uses two synonyms for emphasis. English does not, so brave and daring sounds odd. One strong word is enough.

Punctuation: Arabic uses its own marks ، ؛ ؟. Never leave an English comma or question mark in an Arabic text.

Which Arabic? Use Modern Standard Arabic (الفصحى المعاصرة) for written translation. Use a dialect (العامية) only if the client asks for it.

Key takeaway
English → Arabic: put the verb first and join short sentences.
Arabic → English: put the subject first, drop repeated synonyms and split long sentences.

Meaning, form and context

المعنى والبناء والسياق
Team notes
Concept
Form البناء
The structure of the words: word, phrase, sentence.
Meaning المعنى
The idea those words express.
Denotation المعنى المعجمي
The basic dictionary meaning.
Connotation المعنى الإيحائي
The extra feeling a word carries: positive, negative, respectful.
Key takeaway
Meaning comes before form. المعنى أهمّ من البناء You may change the structure, but never the meaning.
SourceBlair, Bush’s poodle
Targetبلير، تابعُ بوش
Why: “poodle” means nothing political in Arabic. We keep the meaning, “an obedient follower”, and drop the image.

Same meaning, different feeling

dieمات neutral
pass awayتوفّي respectful
kill · assassinateقتل · اغتال political
friend · comradeصديق · رفيق party member

What is really meant?

Sometimes the speaker means more than the words say.

أشعرُ بالحرّ.→“Please turn on the AC.”
It’s freezing in here.→“Close the window.”

Context decides لكلّ مقامٍ مقال

Micro context: the words around the problem.
Macro context: who wrote it, for whom, when and where. The translator ● sits in the middle.

One word can have several meanings. bank can be مصرف or ضفّة النهر. عين can be an eye, a spring or a spy. Only the context tells you which.
