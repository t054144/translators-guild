Level 2 · Applied

المستوى الثاني: التطبيق

Short texts. For each text: (1) fill in the brief (client, purpose, audience), (2) translate it, and (3) write two commentary sentences (Ch. 15).

2a · Human rights textEN → AR
All human beings are born free and equal in dignity and rights. They are endowed with reason and conscience and should act towards one another in a spirit of brotherhood.
Authentic text: United Nations (1948), Universal Declaration of Human Rights, Article 1.
Focus: passive are born · the formal register · compare your version with the official UN Arabic text.
2b · Classical poetryAR → EN
الخيلُ والليلُ والبيداءُ تعرفني  ·  والسيفُ والرمحُ والقرطاسُ والقلمُ
Authentic text: al-Mutanabbi (d. 965 CE), Dīwān.
Focus: keep the rhythm of the list · choose a register for القرطاس · poetry allows more freedom (Ch. 8).
2c · English poetryEN → AR
Shall I compare thee to a summer’s day?
Thou art more lovely and more temperate.
Authentic text: William Shakespeare, Sonnet 18 (1609), lines 1–2.
Focus: summer or spring? Justify your choice with cultural approximation (Ch. 10).
2d · News paragraphEN → AR
The city’s central library will extend its opening hours during the summer holiday, officials announced on Sunday. The library will stay open until 10 p.m. on weekdays, and free workshops on creative writing and translation will also be held.
Practice text written for this handbook in news style. In workshops, use a current authentic news article as well.
Focus: put the verb first · where does officials announced go? · stay open.

Level 3 · Professional

المستوى الثالث: الاحتراف

Full texts. Work through the full cycle, exactly as a professional would.

  1. Analyse the text with the framework on p. .
  2. Translate, choosing a strategy for every problem (Ch. 5–13).
  3. Revise with the checklists on p. .
  4. Write a 150-word commentary (Ch. 15).
  5. Place three risks in the risk grid (Ch. 16), and run the Fairclough checklist (Ch. 17).
3a · News featureEN → AR
Health officials have urged residents to stay indoors between 11 a.m. and 4 p.m. as temperatures are expected to top 50°C this week. “Heatstroke is not something to take lightly,” a Ministry of Health spokesperson said, advising people to drink plenty of water and to check on elderly neighbours. Outdoor workers are most at risk. Meanwhile, shopping malls are expected to be packed as families seek relief from the heat.
Practice text written for this handbook in news style.
Watch for: top 50°C · not something to take lightly (modulation) · check on · packed · numbers and times.
3b · Cultural newsAR → EN
احتفلت الجمعية الثقافية مساءَ أمس بتكريم نخبةٍ من المترجمين الشباب، وذلك في أمسيةٍ حضرها لفيفٌ من الأدباء والأكاديميين. وأكّد رئيس الجمعية في كلمته أنّ الترجمة جسرٌ يصل الشعوب بعضها ببعض، مشيراً إلى أنّ العناية بالمترجمين الشباب واجبٌ لا ترف. واختُتمت الأمسية بقراءاتٍ شعريةٍ مترجمة، وسط أجواءٍ سادها الودّ والتقدير.
Practice text written for this handbook in news style.
Watch for: one long sentence joined by و · the collocation لفيف من · the metaphor جسر · the synonym pair الودّ والتقدير.

إرشادات الإجابة

Translation often has more than one good answer. These are suggestions, not the only correct versions. Any rendering that is accurate, natural and fits the brief is acceptable.

Level 1

#Suggested translationStrategyWhyAlternative
1زارت جدّتها.Collocation“Pay a visit” is just “visit”, not دفعتقامت بزيارة جدّتها
2ظلّت الاتفاقية حبراً على ورق.Functional equivalenceAn Arabic idiom does the same jobلم تُطبَّق الاتفاقية
3يدخّن بشراهة.TranspositionAdjective + noun become verb + adverbialمدخّنٌ شَرِه
4تمطر بغزارة.Idiom → plain meaningThe English image means nothing in Arabicتمطر كأفواه القِرَب

Level 2

2a · Suggestedيُولد جميعُ الناس أحراراً ومتساوين في الكرامة والحقوق، وقد وُهبوا العقلَ والضمير، وعليهم أن يعامل بعضُهم بعضاً بروح الإخاء.
WhyVerb first (يُولد). The two sentences are joined with و. Endowed becomes وُهبوا. Alternative: الوجدان for conscience. Always compare with the official UN Arabic text.
2b · SuggestedThe horses, the night and the desert know me, as do the sword, the spear, the paper and the pen.
WhyThe list of seven nouns is kept, with no added words. القرطاس becomes paper. Alternative: steed and parchment, for a more classical tone.
2c · Suggestedأأشبّهكِ بيومٍ من أيام الربيع؟ أنتِ أبهى منه وأكثرُ اعتدالاً.
WhyCultural approximation: a Gulf reader feels a summer’s day as harsh, but spring as mild and lovely. Alternative: keep الصيف and add a footnote.
2d · Suggestedأعلن مسؤولون يوم الأحد أنّ المكتبة المركزية في المدينة ستمدّد ساعات عملها خلال العطلة الصيفية، إذ ستظلّ مفتوحةً حتى العاشرة مساءً في أيام العمل، كما ستُعقد ورشُ عملٍ مجانية في الكتابة الإبداعية والترجمة.
WhyThe reporting clause moves to the front, as Arabic news does. Stay open becomes تظلّ مفتوحةً. The sentences are joined with إذ and كما.

3a
  • top 50°C → تتجاوز 50 درجة مئوية

3b · Suggested opening
Last night, the Cultural Society honoured a group of outstanding young translators at an evening attended by writers and academics.
  • Split the long sentence
  • Keep the bridge metaphor: the image is shared
  • الودّ والتقدير → a warm, appreciative atmosphere
