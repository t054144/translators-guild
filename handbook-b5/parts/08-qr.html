Checklists

قوائم التحقّق

☐ Before translation

  • Identify the client
  • Identify the purpose
  • Identify the audience
  • Identify the genre
  • Identify the tone
  • Mark the terminology
  • Mark cultural issues
  • Gather your resources

☐ During translation

  • Meaning preserved
  • Terms consistent
  • Register right for the brief
  • Collocations natural
  • Cultural references handled
  • Strategy suits each problem

☐ After translation

  • Accuracy: nothing added or lost
  • Fluency: reads naturally
  • Terminology
  • Grammar and spelling
  • Style and punctuation
  • Formatting
  • Numbers and names
  • Titles and captions
  • Sources verified

☐ Analysis (Ch. 14)

  • Author, publisher and client noted
  • Purpose, audience and genre named
  • Field, tone and style described
  • Key terms and cultural items listed
  • Problems, strategies and resources planned
  • An analysis, not a summary

☐ Commentary (Ch. 15)

  • Problem → solution → strategy → resource → justification
  • Examples quoted from the ST and TT
  • Written in the third person
  • Dictionaries and sources named
  • Analysis and translation agree

☐ Discourse (Ch. 17)

  • Loaded words noticed
  • Hidden doers (passive) noticed
  • Client policy followed
  • Choices explained in the commentary
