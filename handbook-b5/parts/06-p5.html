Genre-based translation

A genre (الجنس النصّي) is a familiar type of text. Each genre has its own rules, so identify the genre first (Ch. 14).

GenreWhat mattersExampleMini task

Translator’s note
News is excellent practice material: it covers every topic and is always up to date. In workshops, bring a current news article and translate it with the text-analysis page (Ch. 14).

The three-check rule: find options in a bilingual dictionary → confirm in a monolingual one → see real use in a parallel text.

The three-check rule in action Example

1 · Bilingualfast colour: the options include سريع، صائم، ثابت
2 · Monolingualثابت = “does not fade”, which fits colours
3 · Parallel textألوان ثابتة appears in Arabic textile texts
Decisionلون ثابت ✓
Common error
Taking the first meaning a bilingual dictionary gives. It is often not the right one for your context.

Machine translation and AI الترجمة الآلية والذكاء الاصطناعي

Concept
Machine translation الترجمة الآلية
Translation done by software, such as neural MT or AI chat tools (LLMs).
Hallucination الهلوسة
AI output that sounds right but is not in the source, such as an added fact or a lost “not”.

When to use machine translation

Useful for
A first draft of simple texts · checking ideas · large volumes
Risky for
Legal, medical and religious texts · idioms and poetry · confidential files

A safe workflow

Typical machine errors EN → AR

ErrorMachine outputCorrection
Literal idiomraining cats and dogs → تمطر قططاً وكلاباًتمطر بغزارة
Wrong genderThe doctor said she… → قال الطبيبقالت الطبيبة
English word orderالحكومة أعلنت…أعلنت الحكومة…
Wrong collocationpay attention → ادفع الانتباهانتبه

Five rules for responsible use

  1. You are responsible for every word you deliver, whatever tool you used.
  2. Protect the client. Never paste private or confidential text into a public AI tool.
  3. Verify terms, names, numbers, dates and every “not”.
  4. Check culture and context. MT often translates idioms word by word.
  5. Be open. Tell the client if you used MT or AI.

Translation quality and post-editing

جودة الترجمة والتحرير اللاحق
Team notes: revision and post-editingAdded framework: ISO 18587 (2017)
Concept
Revision المراجعة
Checking a translation against the source and correcting it.
Post-editing التحرير اللاحق
A human correcting machine translation. The team notes call it the new direction for translators.
Light post-editing
The text is correct and understandable, but not polished. It is for internal use.
Full post-editing
The text is ready to publish, as good as a human translation.

Six quality criteria

CriterionAsk yourself
AccuracyIs the meaning the same? Was anything added or lost?
FluencyDoes it read as if it had been written in the TL?
TerminologyAre the terms correct and consistent with the term list?
GrammarAre agreement, tense, spelling and hamza all correct?
StyleDo the register and tone fit the brief?
FormattingAre numbers, names, titles, captions and punctuation all correct?

Revise in three readings

  1. Accuracy reading, with the ST next to you: check meaning, omissions and additions.
  2. Fluency reading, without the ST: does it sound natural?
  3. Final proofread: numbers, names, punctuation, titles and captions.

A full list of common grammar and style mistakes is on the next page.

Key takeaway
Judge every translation for accuracy, fluency, terminology, grammar, style and formatting, and check every number, name, title and caption. The full checklist is on p. .
