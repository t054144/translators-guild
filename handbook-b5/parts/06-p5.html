Genre-based translation

الترجمة حسب الجنس النصّي
Requested in the team notesExamples written for this handbook, except the poetry line

A genre (الجنس النصّي) is a familiar type of text. Each genre has its own rules, so identify the genre first (Ch. 14).

GenreWhat mattersExampleMini task
NewsالخبرFacts first, neutral tone, short headlinesOil prices rose sharply on Monday.
ارتفعت أسعار النفط ارتفاعاً حادّاً يوم الاثنين.		Write an Arabic headline: Kuwait, Australia sign trade deal
PoliticalالسياسيLoaded words, set diplomatic phrasesThe two sides held frank and constructive talks.
أجرى الجانبان محادثاتٍ صريحةً وبنّاءة.		Compare regime and government (Ch. 17)
LegalالقانونيPrecision, fixed formulas; shall = obligationThis Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Kuwait.
تخضع هذه الاتفاقية لقوانين دولة الكويت.		Translate provided that in a sentence
InstitutionalالرسميOfficial names and titles, formal registerThe Ministry of Foreign Affairs announced…
أعلنت وزارة الخارجية…		Translate Chancellor of the Exchequer
MedicalالطبيZero ambiguity, exact dosesTake one tablet twice daily after meals.
تناوَل قرصاً واحداً مرّتين يومياً بعد الأكل.		Explain psychology vs psychiatry
TechnicalالتقنيClear commands, consistent termsPress and hold the power button for five seconds.
اضغط مطوّلاً على زرّ التشغيل لمدّة خمس ثوانٍ.		Find the Arabic for cybersecurity
AcademicالأكاديميCareful claims, formal styleThe results suggest that…
تشير النتائج إلى أنّ…		Translate This study examines…
LiteraryالأدبيVoice, feeling, natural dialogueIt has been a long day.
كان يوماً شاقّاً. (not طويلاً)		Translate He was over the moon.
PoetryالشعرImage, rhythm, beauty; more freedomالخيلُ والليلُ والبيداءُ تعرفني
The horses, the night and the desert know me (al-Mutanabbi)		Keep the rhythm of the list
AdvertisingالإعلانيPersuasion, rhythm, wordplaySave more. Smile more.
وفّر أكثر… وابتسم أكثر!		Keep the parallel structure
Translator’s note
News is excellent practice material: it covers every topic and is always up to date. In workshops, bring a current news article and translate it with the text-analysis page (Ch. 14).

Dictionaries, resources and AI/MT

المعاجم المطبوعة والرقمية والترجمة الآلية
Requested in the team notesAdded framework: resource guide and MT basics
Concept
Bilingual dictionary معجم ثنائي اللغة
Gives equivalents. Use it to find options.
Monolingual dictionary معجم أحادي اللغة
Explains words in one language. Use it to confirm the meaning.

What to look for in a dictionary entry

DefinitionsWhich sense fits?
CollocationsWhich words go with it?
RegisterFormal or informal?
DomainLegal, medical…?
Usage notesWarnings, limits
ExamplesReal sentences
ReliabilityWho made it?
DateIs it current?
ResourceBest for
Al-Mawrid (English–Arabic, Arabic–English)Everyday vocabulary in both directions
Hans Wehr, A Dictionary of Modern Written ArabicArabic words organised by root
Al-Muʿjam al-Wasīṭ المعجم الوسيطConfirming Arabic meanings
Oxford Collocations DictionaryFinding natural English word partners
Online dictionaries (e.g. Almaany)Quick look-ups. Always double-check them.
UNTERM · ArabtermOfficial and technical terms

The three-check rule: find options in a bilingual dictionary → confirm in a monolingual one → see real use in a parallel text.

Machine translation and AI الترجمة الآلية والذكاء الاصطناعي

Concept
Machine translation الترجمة الآلية
Translation done by software, such as neural MT or AI chat tools (LLMs).
Hallucination الهلوسة
AI output that sounds right but is not in the source, such as an added fact or a lost “not”.
Translator’s note
You are responsible for every word you deliver, whatever tool you used. Check the terms, context, culture, names, numbers and negatives, and never paste a client’s private text into a public AI tool.

Translation quality and post-editing

جودة الترجمة والتحرير اللاحق
Team notes: revision and post-editingAdded framework: ISO 18587 (2017)
Concept
Revision المراجعة
Checking a translation against the source and correcting it.
Post-editing التحرير اللاحق
A human correcting machine translation. The team notes call it the new direction for translators.
Light post-editing
The text is correct and understandable, but not polished. It is for internal use.
Full post-editing
The text is ready to publish, as good as a human translation.

When to use machine translation

Useful for
A first draft of simple texts · checking ideas · large volumes
Risky for
Legal, medical and religious texts · idioms and poetry · confidential files
