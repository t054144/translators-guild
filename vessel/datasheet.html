Still on the table

Where this goes next

This page is one direction built far enough to judge. Here's the rest of the shortlist, and what's already load-bearing in what you're looking at.

01

The dot field is the interface

Four modes off one 325-dot engine: punchcard scroll, name-derived sigil, Conway's Game of Life, and a paintable surface. Nothing on the page duplicates it.

02

Deterministic engraving

FNV-1a over the letters seeds an xorshift PRNG, mirrored down the centre column so the result reads woven rather than random. Your name always makes your pattern.

03

The shared wall

Supabase is already in this repo. Every dot anyone paints persists for the next visitor, and a global sip counter ticks up on click. Live shared state is what makes people send the link on.

04

The night shift

Your actual photo — 11pm, blurred laptops, someone still working behind you — with the bokeh rebuilt in CSS. The section an engineer can't write.

05

Spring-physics liquid

Fill level follows scroll on a damped spring, wobbles, settles. Plus a synthesised stainless-steel clink from Web Audio oscillators — no audio file.

06

Per-name share cards

Engrave a name, get a URL. The link preview shows that person's mug with their own sigil on it. This is the mechanism that actually spreads.
