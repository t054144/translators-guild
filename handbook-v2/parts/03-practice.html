Answers

الإجابات

These are suggested answers. Often there is more than one good translation: any version that is accurate, natural and right for the reader is acceptable.

Level 1Sentences

#Suggested answerWhy
1زارت جدّتها.“Pay a visit” simply means “visit”.
2ظلّت الاتفاقية حبراً على ورق.A functional equivalent (Chapter 7).
3يدخّن بشراهة.Transposition: the adjective becomes a verb.
4تمطر بغزارة.An idiom is never translated word by word.
5أصدرت وزارة الداخلية البريطانية بياناً.The Home Office is the interior ministry.
6He came back empty-handed.The proverb’s meaning, in natural English.
7The news warmed my heart.Arabic “cold” relief becomes English “warmth”.
8He is very brave.English does not need two synonyms.
9I am growing worried about the results.A natural English collocation.
10The minister resigned yesterday.Subject first; قدّم استقالته = “resigned”.
11انتبه من فضلك.Collocation
12أعلنت الحكومة إجراءاتٍ جديدة.Start with the verb.
13لديّ ثلاثةُ إخوة.Numbers 3–10 take the opposite gender.
14Life is beautiful.No “the” before general ideas.
15He married her last year.No Arabic preposition in English.

Level 2Short texts

2a. يُولد جميعُ الناس أحراراً ومتساوين في الكرامة والحقوق، وقد وُهبوا العقلَ والضمير، وعليهم أن يعامل بعضُهم بعضاً بروح الإخاء.
Start with the verb (يُولد) and join the two sentences with و. Then compare with the official UN Arabic text.

2b. The horses, the night and the desert know me, as do the sword, the spear, the paper and the pen.
Keep the list of seven nouns. Steed and parchment would give a more classical tone.

2c. أأشبّهكِ بيومٍ من أيام الربيع؟ أنتِ أبهى منه وأكثرُ اعتدالاً.
Spring gives Arab readers the same pleasant feeling that summer gives English readers (Chapter 12).

Level 3Tips

3a. top 50°C → تتجاوز 50 درجة مئوية · not something to take lightly → أمرٌ خطير (modulation) · check on → الاطمئنان على · packed → مكتظّة

3b. Suggested opening: Last night, the Cultural Society honoured a group of outstanding young translators at an evening attended by writers and academics. Split the long sentence, keep the “bridge” image, and translate الودّ والتقدير as a warm, appreciative atmosphere.
