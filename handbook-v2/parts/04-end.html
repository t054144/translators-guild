Checklists

قوائم التحقّق

Use these checklists with every translation. Tick each point as you go.

Before you translate

  • I know the client, the purpose and the audience.
  • I have read the whole text twice.
  • I know the genre and the tone.
  • I have marked difficult words, idioms, names and cultural items.
  • I have made a list of key terms.
  • I have my dictionaries and resources ready.

While you translate

  • The meaning is the same as in the source.
  • The terms are the same all the way through.
  • The style suits the brief (formal or informal).
  • The collocations and idioms sound natural.
  • Cultural references are handled.

After you translate

  • Nothing is missing and nothing is added.
  • Every “not” is still there.
  • Grammar, spelling and punctuation are correct.
  • Numbers, names, dates, titles and captions are checked.
  • I have read the translation once without the source.

Your commentary

  • For each problem: problem → solution → strategy → resource → justification.
  • I quote examples from the source and the translation.
  • I write in the third person.

Glossary

The key terms in this handbook, in alphabetical order, with the page where each one is explained.

AdaptationالتكييفReplacing a cultural situation with a familiar one.
AdditionالإضافةAdding words the reader needs.
Analysis (text analysis)تحليل النصStudying a text before translating it.
ApproximationالتقريبBringing the meaning as close as possible to the TL.
BorrowingالاقتراضTaking a word from the SL as it is.
CalqueالنسخTranslating each part of an expression.
ClientالعميلThe person or organisation who asks for a translation.
Collocationالتلازم اللفظيWords that naturally go together.
Commentaryالتعليق على الترجمةA text that explains and justifies translation choices.
Commissioned translatorمترجم بتكليفA translator who works for a client.
CommunicationالتواصلUnderstanding and being understood.
Connotationالمعنى الإيحائيThe feeling a word carries.
ContextالسياقThe words and situation around a text.
Cultural approximationالتقريب الثقافيBringing cultural meaning close to the TL reader.
Cultural substitutionالإبدال الثقافيReplacing a cultural reference with a TL one.
Denotationالمعنى المعجميThe basic dictionary meaning.
Descriptive translationالترجمة الوصفيةExplaining a word by describing it.
Discourse analysisتحليل الخطابStudying how language shows power and ideology.
DomesticationالتوطينMaking a text feel local to the reader.
EquivalenceالتكافؤA TL expression that matches the SL one.
ForeignisationالتغريبKeeping the foreign flavour of a text.
FormالبناءThe structure of words and sentences.
Formal equivalenceالتكافؤ الشكليKeeps the form of the original.
Free translationالترجمة الحرّةKeeps the message, but not the form.
Functional equivalenceالتكافؤ الوظيفيKeeps the function of the original.
Genreالجنس النصّيA type of text, e.g. news or a contract.
HallucinationالهلوسةAI output that is not in the source.
Ideational equivalenceالتكافؤ الفكريKeeps only the basic idea.
IdeologyالأيديولوجياBeliefs that affect how events are described.
Idiomالتعبير الاصطلاحيA fixed expression whose meaning is not the sum of its words.
Interpretationالترجمة الشفهيةTranslating speech.
Lexical creationالنحت المعجميCreating a new word in the TL.
Literal translationالترجمة الحرفيةKeeping the words and changing only the grammar.
Machine translationالترجمة الآليةTranslation done by software.
MeaningالمعنىThe idea that words express.
MetaphorالاستعارةA comparison without “as” or “like”.
Modern Standard Arabicالفصحى المعاصرةThe Arabic of newspapers, laws and education.
ModulationالتحويرChanging the point of view.
OmissionالحذفLeaving out words that repeat the meaning.
Post-editingالتحرير اللاحقA human correcting machine translation.
ProcedureالإجراءA technique for translating a word or phrase.
ProverbالمثلA traditional saying.
Registerالمستوى اللغويHow formal the language is.
RevisionالمراجعةChecking and correcting a translation.
RiskالخطرThe chance that a choice causes a problem.
Self-initiated translatorمترجم بمبادرة ذاتيةA translator who chooses a text, with no client.
SimileالتشبيهA comparison with “as” or “like”.
Source language (SL)اللغة المصدرThe language of the original text.
StrategyالاستراتيجيةA planned solution to a translation problem.
Target language (TL)اللغة الهدفThe language of the translation.
TermالمصطلحA word with a special meaning in one field.
Translationالترجمة التحريريةTransferring the meaning of a written text.
Translation briefموجز الترجمةClient, purpose and audience.
Transliterationالنقل الصوتيWriting a word’s sounds in the other alphabet.
TranspositionالإبدالChanging the word class, e.g. noun → verb.
Verbal sentenceالجملة الفعليةA sentence that starts with a verb.
